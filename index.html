How to play

WASD move · Double-tap W sprint · Shift crouch · Space jump · Click fire · Right-click aim · R reload · 1–5 or mouse wheel to switch weapons · Tab scoreboard · M music on/off · Esc pause · change controls in Settings · F3 bugfix overlay (hold F3 + B boxes, H hitboxes, N nav grid, C copy a bug report)

On a phone or tablet: your left thumb moves (push all the way forward to sprint), drag anywhere on the right to look, and use the on-screen buttons for everything else. You can drag on FIRE to aim while shooting.

Killstreaks: pick 3 before each match (all modes except Gun Derby and Zombies). Kills in a row without going down earn them: 🔭 Scout’s Eye (2), 🥕 Feed Bag (3), 🌾 Haymaker (4), 📯 Herd Call (5), 🐎 Thoroughbred (6), 🐴 Stampede (7), 🚁 Pony Express (8), 🌩️ Kirin Storm (9), 🌟 Final Gallop (10). Use them with Z, X and C; a secret fourth appears at a 20-kill streak and goes on V. Marks: a marked girl dies to any Hay Bale Cannon headshot. Modes: Free Play (a sandbox with your own rules: any number of bots or none, win by kills, time or never, weapons on or off, Lucky Chest weapons, infinite ammo, god mode; no XP or stats) · Zombie Waves (co-op survival against waves of android zombies; kills earn points, and 950 points opens the map’s Lucky Chest (press F) for a powerful wonder weapon with limited ammo) · Zombies (survivors with a shotgun and pistol must last 3 minutes; zombies have no guns; one swipe scratches a survivor and a second turns her. Zombies win if they turn everyone) · Team Deathmatch (5v5, first team to 30 kills) · Free-for-All (everyone for herself, first to 15 kills) · Gun Derby (free-for-all; every kill gives you the next weapon, two laps of all five, a kill with the last one wins). Those are the defaults: change the Win target on the match setup screen (or in an online room, if you’re the host) to play first to 5 kills, a 10-minute Zombies round, one quick Gun Derby lap and so on · Lucky Horseshoes (5v5; grab enemy horseshoes to score, your team’s to deny) · Winner’s Circle (5v5; hold the moving circle to score).

Story: choose Story on the title screen. Each chapter unlocks when you finish the one before it. Cutscenes play between missions (click, tap, Space or Enter to advance; Esc or Skip scene to skip). Missions have a primary objective (waves to defeat, places to reach, targets to destroy or areas to hold) and a secondary one, and the HUD shows what to do next. Online: choose Play online on the title screen, create a room and send your friends its 4-letter code or invite link. Up to 4 players share a room and play any mode. The host picks the mode, the map, how many bots join (none, for players only, up to a full 10 racers) and the bots’ skill. Abilities work online too.
