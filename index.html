Hoshimori Racing Academy 星森学園

An elite boarding school for horse-girl racers, built around its famous oval track. At sunset the cherry blossoms fall and the dorm rivalries turn into all-out team battles.

  • School & clock tower: north edge, red team spawns in front of it
  • Racetrack & starting gate: open centre with infield crates
  • Grandstand: cover in front of the blue spawn
  • Sakura courtyard & shrine: torii gate, stone lanterns, koi pond
  • Dormitory: east side, balconies and vending machines
  • Gymnasium & stables: flanking routes west and south

How to play

WASD move · Double-tap W sprint · Shift crouch · Space jump · Click fire · Right-click aim · R reload · 1–5 or mouse wheel to switch weapons · Tab scoreboard · M music on/off · Esc pause · change controls in Settings

On a phone or tablet: your left thumb moves (push all the way forward to sprint), drag anywhere on the right to look, and use the on-screen buttons for everything else. You can drag on FIRE to aim while shooting.

Killstreaks: pick 3 before each match (all modes except Gun Derby and Zombies). Kills in a row without going down earn them: 🔭 Scout’s Eye (2), 🌾 Haymaker (4), 🐎 Thoroughbred (6), 🚁 Pony Express (8), 🌟 Final Gallop (10). Use them with Z, X and C. Modes: Zombies (survivors with a shotgun and pistol must last 3 minutes; zombies have no guns; one swipe scratches a survivor and a second turns her. Zombies win if they turn everyone) · Team Deathmatch (5v5, first team to 30 kills) · Free-for-All (everyone for herself, first to 15 kills) · Gun Derby (free-for-all; every kill gives you the next weapon, two laps of all five, a kill with the last one wins) · Lucky Horseshoes (5v5; grab enemy horseshoes to score, your team’s to deny) · Winner’s Circle (5v5; hold the moving circle to score).

Online: choose Play online on the title screen, create a room and send your friends its 4-letter code or invite link. Up to 4 players share a room and play any of the five modes. The host picks the mode, the map, how many bots join (none, for players only, up to a full 10 racers) and the bots’ skill. Abilities are off in online matches for now.
